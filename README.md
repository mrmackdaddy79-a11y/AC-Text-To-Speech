🚀 AC Text to Speech v2.0 - The UI Overhaul
This massive update transforms the plugin with a fully featured Virindi UI, smart chat filtering, and quality-of-life engine upgrades.

🌟 Features & Upgrades:

Full Virindi Interface: Control your audio directly in-game. Toggle audio independently for Direct Tells, Fellowship, NPCs/Vendors, and General Chat.

Per-Character Memory: Your Master Audio (ON/OFF) setting is now saved individually per character. Mute your mules once, and they will stay completely silent on all future logins.

Smart Name Memory: If a player or NPC sends you multiple lines in a row, the plugin remembers them and skips repeating their name for 15 seconds.

Spam & Command Silencer: Check the "Block Identical Repeats" box to ignore identical back-to-back chat spam. The engine also intelligently ignores hidden bot commands (messages starting with ;, /, !, or @) and silences the "joined channel" spam upon login.

No Spell-Cast Spam: Intelligently ignores local chat to keep repetitive spell words and combat spam out of your ears.

Clean Audio: Automatically strips out hidden formatting so the dialogue sounds completely natural.

Customizable Voices: Runs efficiently on the built-in Windows Text-to-Speech engine. To change the accent, voice, or speed, simply adjust the default text-to-speech settings in your Windows OS!

Multitasking Friendly: Hear when your allegiance or a buyer needs you, even if you are alt-tabbed out of the game.

## Installation
1. Go to the **Releases** section on the right side of the GitHub page.
2. Download the latest `ACTextToSpeech.dll` file.
3. Place the downloaded `.dll` into your `Decal Plugins` folder.
4. Open Decal, click **Add**, and browse to the `.dll` to register it.
